I have been a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Dallas (UT Dallas) since fall 2025, under the guidance of Prof. Feng Chen (Fellow, Eugene McDermott Distinguished Professor) and Prof. Bingzhe Li (Assistant Professor).

My research interests focus on computer-use agents and AI safety — building agents that automate computer work and understanding how to make them trustworthy and reliable.
